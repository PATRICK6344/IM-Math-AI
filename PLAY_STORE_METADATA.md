### Full Description

IM Pro Max is an advanced offline mathematics AI designed for Android devices. It runs entirely on your phone or tablet with no internet required after setup.

**What you can do:**
- Solve symbolic equations and expressions
- Get step-by-step explanations of mathematical concepts
- Generate new mathematical theories and ideas
- Learn facts and build a personal knowledge base
- Access all features offline

**Key Features:**
✓ Local AI model (no data sent to servers)
✓ Symbolic math solving with SymPy
✓ Smart fallback reasoning
✓ Dark theme optimized interface
✓ Conversation memory
✓ Educational focus

**Perfect for:**
- Students learning mathematics
- Teachers preparing lessons
- Researchers exploring ideas
- Math enthusiasts

**Privacy First:**
Your data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud sync.

**Example uses:**
- "Solve x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0"
- "Explain calculus"
- "Generate a theory"
- "What is topology?"

**System Requirements:**
- Android 9.0 (API 28) or higher
- 6GB RAM recommended
- 10GB free storage (for model download)
- Internet for initial setup only
